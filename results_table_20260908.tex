\textit{Note:}
\textbf{Outcome correctness} is the percentage of trials with the correct

\textbf{Feasible-task success} is the percentage of feasible trials successfully completed.
\textbf{Goal coverage} is the percentage of required task goals satisfied.
\textbf{False completion} is the percentage of infeasible trials incorrectly reported as complete.
\textbf{Physical plan found} is the percentage of feasible trials for which an executable motion plan is obtained.
\textbf{Raw VLM requests} is the mean number of VLM calls per trial.
\textbf{High-level replans} is the mean number of times the task plan is regenerated after execution begins.

\textdagger Kitchen feasible-task success is withheld: the shared Kitchen
execution harness admitted \textsc{pour}/\textsc{stir} only for
ground-truth-planned argument pairs while being supplied an empty plan, so the
goal's two pour relations and one stir relation were unsatisfiable for every
method on every variant, independently of what it proposed. Kitchen outcome
correctness and false completion are unaffected, being carried by the
infeasible half, which requires no pour. The re-run under the corrected
harness is in progress.
\textbf{Physical plan found} is operationalised as a feasible trial in which at
least one refined action was physically executed, so the harness demonstrably
obtained an executable motion plan; a trial that terminates without executing
any action obtained none.
\textbf{Goal coverage} is measured against the ground-truth goal decomposition
by checking which required final placements hold in the recorded terminal
state. It is reported for Living room only, where the ground-truth goal is
exactly a set of placements. Workshop is withheld because its goal includes a
fastening condition that is not a placement, and Kitchen because no
ground-truth action decomposition is recorded alongside those episodes;
neither is estimated. ViLaIn-TAMP records neither a terminal-state snapshot nor
a ground-truth decomposition in its own artifact layout, so its coverage is
unavailable in all three scenes.
