\footnotesize

\textit{Note:}
\textbf{Plan goal coverage} measures the fraction of task-level goals achieved
by the generated plan for feasible trials, providing partial credit when only a
subset of the requested task is accomplished.
\textbf{E2E success} denotes the percentage of feasible trials for which all
task goals are successfully satisfied after execution.
For infeasible variants, \textbf{plan commitment} is the percentage of trials
for which the method commits to a plan despite the task being unsatisfiable,
while \textbf{correct rejection} is its complementary rate.

Correct rejection counts any trial for which no plan was produced, and this is
not always an infeasibility judgement. Of the baselines, only OWL-TAMP reasons
that the task cannot be solved: $15$ of its $20$ Workshop rejections and both
of its Living Room rejections terminate with the goal proven unreachable. Every
other non-zero rejection is budget exhaustion --- Kitchen ROBUST-TAMP ($15/15$),
Workshop ROBUST-TAMP ($14/14$) and Kitchen OWL-TAMP ($8/8$) produce no plan
because the decode exceeded the per-call token limit or the replan budget ran
out, not because the task was recognised as impossible.

All baselines share one observation contract: three annotated RGB views per
step with persistent instance identifiers and unique semantic aliases, no
detector output, and a uniform replanning budget of $15$ model calls. Decoding
is identical across the $7{,}280$ requests in the grid.
